From Orchard Harvest to Chilled Dispatch

Harvest, storage, dispatch, and spoilage records are traditionally disconnected, hiding avoidable losses. The Harvest Lot Register establishes an unbroken chain of custody: tracking real-time condition degradation, aging thresholds, split/merge provenance, and root-cause loss accountability.
